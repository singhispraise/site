About Sing His Praise

Why Sing His Praise exists and what shapes this community.

Sing His Praise exists to connect and equip worship leaders and musicians serving in local churches. It is built around the belief that worship leaders and team members grow stronger when they have access to shared resources, collective wisdom, biblical encouragement, and authentic fellowship with others carrying similar responsibility.

Christ-Centered Worship

We exist first and foremost to glorify Jesus Christ. Worship is not performance or preference, but a response to who God is and what He has done.

Kingdom Mindset

We set our minds on things above and labor with a kingdom perspective, remembering that our service is ultimately for Christ and for the good of His Church.

Biblical Foundation

Our practices, language, and theology of worship are rooted in Scripture and shaped by God’s Word, not by trends, personality, or preference.

Community

We are called to encourage one another and build one another up. Sing His Praise is meant to be a place of authentic fellowship, shared wisdom, and mutual strengthening.

Growth

We want to keep learning. As worship leaders and musicians, we pursue growth in skill, understanding, maturity, and the wisdom needed to serve well.

Next Generation

We invest in and encourage upcoming worship leaders and musicians, helping them grow in confidence, calling, and capability for the years ahead.

Excellence

We desire to offer our best to God. Excellence is not perfectionism, but faithful stewardship of the gifts, opportunities, and responsibilities He has given us.

Go

We want our worship and our lives to serve the mission of God. As we grow, we also go, carrying the gospel, strengthening the Church, and serving where there is need.

Gatherings

Upcoming events

We want to provide gatherings that create real moments for learning, encouragement, and connection. The purpose is to help people not only receive resources, but also build confidence, ask honest questions, and grow through shared experience with others who understand their context.

New member welcome call

A live orientation for new members to meet leaders, hear the vision, ask questions, and understand how Sing His Praise can support their church context.

Worship leaders roundtable

A facilitated conversation around leading teams with health, humility, biblical grounding, and musical clarity in the life of the local church.

Featured training release

A teaching release with notes, follow-up prompts, and conversation starters that help members apply what they are learning in real ministry settings.
